How it works

From hello to a real date, in four easy steps

Olygo keeps things simple, so you can spend less time in the app and more time getting to know someone.

    Sign up with Google, Apple or email. Add your best photos, a short bio and what you’re looking for.

    Like, pass or super like. When the feeling is mutual — it’s a match.

    Chat in real time, share photos and plan your first meet-up — at your own pace.

FAQ

Questions? We’ve got answers.

Is Olygo free to use?

Yes. Creating a profile, swiping, matching and chatting are free. Optional premium features add extras like read receipts and more photos.

How does profile verification work?

You verify your phone or email and can complete a quick photo verification. Verified profiles get a badge so you know who is real.

Why is there a daily swipe limit?

Olygo gives you a set number of swipes each day so you slow down, look properly and focus on people who genuinely match what you want.

Can I block or report someone?

Yes. You can block or report any profile or conversation in a couple of taps. Blocked users can no longer see you or message you.

Can I take a break without deleting my account?

Yes. You can deactivate your account temporarily and reactivate it any time by logging back in. If you prefer, you can also delete your account permanently from Settings.
