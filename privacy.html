Privacy policy

Last updated: 10 November 2026

Overview

Olygo (“we”, “us”) helps people meet through genuine, verified connections. This policy explains what information we collect when you use the Olygo apps and website, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

  • Account details — name, email address, phone number, date of birth and gender, or the basic profile shared by Google or Apple when you sign in with them.
  • Profile content — photos, bio, interests, lifestyle answers and dating preferences you choose to add.
  • Verification data — information used to confirm your phone, email or photo verification.
  • Location — approximate location to show you people nearby, with your permission.
  • Activity — likes, passes, matches, messages and reports, so the service can work and stay safe.
  • Device data — device type, app version and push-notification tokens.

How we use it

  • To create and run your account and show your profile to potential matches.
  • To match you with people based on your preferences.
  • To deliver messages and notifications.
  • To keep the community safe — verifying profiles, reviewing reports and preventing abuse.
  • To improve the app and fix issues.

We do not sell your personal information.

Who can see your information

Other members can see the profile information you choose to share. Your exact location, email and phone number are never shown to other members. We share data with service providers (such as hosting, email and push-notification providers) only as needed to run Olygo, and when required by law.

Your choices

  • Edit your profile, photos and preferences at any time in the app.
  • Control discovery and who can message you in Privacy & Safety settings.
  • Turn location and notifications on or off in your device settings.
  • Block or report any member.

Deactivating or deleting your account

You can temporarily deactivate your account from Profile → Settings → Account and reactivate it by logging back in. You can also delete your account permanently from the same screen; we then remove your profile and personal data, except where we must keep limited records for safety or legal reasons. If you can’t access the app, email privacy@olygo.app from your registered address.

Security

We use encrypted connections, access controls and careful data handling to protect your information. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong, unique password and report anything suspicious.

Age requirement

Olygo is only for adults aged 18 and over. We remove accounts that we believe belong to minors.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. If changes are significant, we’ll let you know in the app.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Email privacy@olygo.app.
