Safety

Dating safety tips

Simple habits that help you meet people with confidence.

Online

  • Look for the verified badge. Verified members have confirmed their identity with a photo check.
  • Keep chats in Olygo until you trust someone. Our reporting tools only work inside the app.
  • Never send money or share financial details, no matter how convincing the story.
  • Protect personal info like your home address, workplace or daily routine.
  • Trust your instincts. If something feels off, block and report — it takes two taps.

Meeting in person

  • Meet in a busy public place, and arrange your own transport.
  • Tell a friend where you’re going, who you’re meeting and when you’ll be back.
  • Stay in control of your drinks and belongings.
  • It’s always OK to leave early.

Reporting

Open the person’s profile or your chat with them, tap the menu and choose Report or Block. Our team reviews every report. If you are in immediate danger, contact local emergency services (112 in India).

Need help?

Email our safety team at safety@olygo.app.
